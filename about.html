Who we are

Two co-founders. One team. We run your marketing like it's our own.

Cherry Content is run end-to-end by Sash and Valentina. No account-manager handoffs. No "your contact is on leave." You always know who's doing your marketing — because it's us, every time.

Why we started this

Small businesses get sold expensive marketing all the time. Most of it doesn't work.

Vague reports. Stock photos. Ads that never convert. Social calendars that go quiet two months in. We've seen it happen to people we know — friends, family, clients of clients. We started Cherry Content to be the opposite: a team that actually shoots your photos, runs your social, builds your ads, and tells you in plain English what's working and what isn't.

Valentina runs the day-to-day — Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok, brand, captions, scheduling, client comms. Sash runs the numbers side — Meta ads, dashboards, conversion analysis — plus the photography studio (Pics By Sash) that does the shoots. Two people, two specialties, one team. That's the whole staff card.

We're small on purpose. We take on businesses we think we can actually move the needle for, and we tell you on the audit call if that's not you.

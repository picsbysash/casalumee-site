If you turn on the AI add-on

Three steps. No lead lost.

1

The lead arrives

Someone messages your WhatsApp, clicks your Meta ad, DMs your Instagram, or texts your business number. The system catches it in seconds and opens a record.

2

It drafts. You approve.

A reply is drafted with full context, pinged to your WhatsApp. One tap to approve, edit, or skip. You stay in control — the AI just saves you the typing.

3

Tracked end-to-end

Every message, email and follow-up is logged against the customer and moves through your pipeline — from first contact to booked-and-done. Nothing slips.

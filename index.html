How the AI Lead System Works

Three steps. Zero leaks.

1

The Lead Arrives

A customer messages your WhatsApp, clicks your Meta Ad, or texts your business number. The system captures every detail instantly and opens a new record.

2

AI Drafts. You Approve.

An applied-intelligence reply is generated with full context, then pinged to your WhatsApp. One tap to approve, edit, or override — you remain in command at every step.

3

Tracked to Completion

Every message, email and task is logged against the customer. Move them through your pipeline from first contact to delivered engagement — with full audit visibility.
